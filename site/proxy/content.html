Is the web proxy free to use?

Yes. The current free allowance is up to 30 minutes and 100 MiB of traffic per visitor, per day. Your remaining time and data appear in the browser toolbar. Ending a session or changing country does not reset your allowance.

What can I use an online web proxy for?

Use it to preview a public website from another country, check how your own pages appear in a different location, or try browsing through a proxy without installing software. Availability and content can vary by website.

Is a web proxy the same as a VPN?

No. This web proxy routes the website traffic inside this page. It does not cover your other browser tabs or applications. A VPN can route traffic from more of your device, depending on its configuration.

Will every website work?

Some websites, video services, downloads, and sign-in flows may not work through a web proxy. Websites can also restrict proxy traffic. This tool does not guarantee anonymity; a website can still recognize information you provide or an account you sign into.

Can I change country while browsing?

Yes. Choose another location from the toolbar. Changing country starts a fresh website session, so you may need to sign in again. Your daily time and data allowance stays the same.
